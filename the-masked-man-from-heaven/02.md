Philikos, the king of fire, and Kalos, the king of water, were never adversaries, according to history. The vendors selling at the border exchanged greetings.

Coal and charcoal are produced in the fire nation. Mine carriage's rails are completely stretched. Those who produce swords and armor, armor and jewelry. It's no surprise that this country has grown so powerful.

The country of the sea is a suburban one. Agriculture and plantations abound across its enormous landmass. It can also be found in a number of places, some of which are even administered by the fire nation community.

It was not uncommon for people to talk about Fotia, the princess of the fire kingdom, at the market. They merely know the name and don't bother to look.

Filikos established himself as a strict father by refusing to let him leave the palace until the scheduled time.

They compared themselves to Gennaios, the water prince who had vanished into thin air. Between those who do not desire self, there is a strong idea that he is only transient.

Gennaios is well-known in both countries for his generosity in the marketplace. He's frequently seen pushing a mining cart and standing in one of the stores, assisting with the sale of farm produce.

"Evdaimonia has no right to be queen!"

"If Gennaios had still been alive, I would have made him king!"

When Doulos declared himself king of fire, the peace was broken.

People's negative thoughts are sparked temporarily by the belief that the water nation is to blame for what is currently occurring.

Despite the fact that Gennaios was the supplier of the wine that Doulos had always enjoyed, his business was demolished. Doulos was caught stealing a number of times, but Gennaios always forgave him.
